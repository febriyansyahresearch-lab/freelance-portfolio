## Methodology Notes

All projects follow a consistent practitioner-oriented methodology:
1. **Problem formulation** grounded in real industry scenarios
2. **Synthetic data generation** for reproducible experimentation
3. **Baseline implementation** with established ML/security techniques
4. **API-first design** for production deployment readiness
5. **Evaluation** with industry-standard metrics
